We do a lot of solar work in Melkbosstrand, including large systems on slate roofs and neat inverter and battery installs inside the house.

Being right on the coast, Melkbos is hard on outdoor equipment. Salty air speeds up corrosion, so we pay extra attention to mounting hardware, sealed isolators and cable glands on the roof and outside walls. Slate roofs also need careful handling, so we work carefully and replace any tiles we lift.

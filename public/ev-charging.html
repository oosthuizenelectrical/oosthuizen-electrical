Why a wall charger

Faster, safer charging than a wall plug

The cable that comes with most EVs plugs into a normal socket, but it charges slowly and puts a long, heavy load on a circuit not designed for it. A wall charger on its own circuit charges faster and is built for the job.

Questions

EV charging FAQs

What does an EV charger installation involve?

A dedicated circuit from your DB, a local isolator, neat mounting and testing. Read our full EV charger installation guide.

Which EV chargers do you install?

We have installed Victron, BYD, Geely, Jetour, iCAR and Deye chargers. We can install the charger that came with your car, or supply a charger on request.

Do I need to upgrade my DB?

Not always. We check your board and supply during the site visit and tell you upfront if anything needs upgrading.

Can I charge my car from my solar system?

In many cases, yes, depending on your inverter, panels and batteries. We will look at your setup and advise.

Do you install commercial EV chargers?

Yes. We install charging points for businesses, offices, shops, guesthouses and complexes, including several chargers at one site. We check the building's supply and load first so the chargers don't overload it. Three-phase installs are done with our registered three-phase partner.

Can you install a charger in a complex or apartment block?

Yes. We have installed chargers in shared basement parking. Send us the details of your bay and where the nearest supply is, and we will advise. You may need permission from your body corporate first.

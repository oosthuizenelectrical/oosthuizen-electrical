Electrical COCs

How a COC works

An electrical Certificate of Compliance confirms your installation is safe and up to standard. You usually need one when selling a property. Read our COC guide.

  1. Inspection

    We inspect the whole electrical installation at the property.

  2. Report

    You get a written report of anything that doesn't comply.

  3. Quote & repairs

    We quote for the repairs and do the work once you approve.

  4. Certificate

    Once everything complies, we issue your COC.
